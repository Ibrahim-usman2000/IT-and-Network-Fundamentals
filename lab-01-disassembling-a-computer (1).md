## Objective
Understand the safe, correct order for disassembling a desktop computer, identify each major internal component, and describe its function and removal process.

## Tools Needed
- Phillips-head screwdriver (most common)
- Anti-static wrist strap (or frequent grounding by touching the case)
- Small parts tray or container (for screws)
- Flashlight (for dim case interiors)

## Safety Precautions
- Power off the system and unplug it from the wall — never work on a live machine.
- Press the power button once after unplugging to discharge residual power.
- Wear an anti-static wrist strap, or regularly touch an unpainted metal part of the case to discharge static (ESD can silently damage components).
- Lay components on a non-conductive, flat surface once removed.
- Handle cards and boards by their edges — avoid touching gold connectors or chips.

## Disassembly Order & Notes

### 1. Remove the Case Panel(s)
- Unscrew and slide off the side panel(s) to expose the interior.
- _Note what you'd expect to see: motherboard, drives, cables, PSU._

### 2. Disconnect and Remove Storage Drives (HDD/SSD/Optical)
- Disconnect SATA data cable and power cable.
- Unscrew from the drive bay, slide out.
- _Why removed early: frees space and cable clutter for the next steps._

### 3. Remove Expansion Cards (GPU, sound card, etc.)
- Unscrew the bracket at the back of the case.
- Release the PCIe slot latch, then pull the card straight up/out.

### 4. Remove RAM
- Push down the retention clips on both ends of the slot.
- The module pops up slightly — lift straight out.

### 5. Disconnect and Remove the Power Supply (PSU)
- Disconnect all cables (24-pin motherboard, CPU 4/8-pin, drive power, GPU power).
- Unscrew the PSU from the back of the case, slide out.

### 6. Remove the Motherboard
- Disconnect remaining cables (front panel connectors, fans, USB headers).
- Unscrew the standoff screws holding the board to the case.
- Lift the board out carefully.

### 7. (Optional) Remove CPU and Heatsink/Fan
- Unclip or unscrew the heatsink/fan assembly.
- Release the CPU socket lever and lift the CPU straight out — never drag it.
